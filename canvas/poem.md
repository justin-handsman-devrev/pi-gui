# The Code That Grew

A seed of thought, a blinking cursor's glow,
A blank page waiting for the words to flow.
We type, we pause, we delete, we rewrite—
Chasing the perfect line into the night.

The screen hums soft, a patient, steady friend,
As characters cascade without an end.
A semicolon here, a closing brace,
And slowly, logic settles into place.

For every bug that made us pull our hair,
There's a moment when it *just works* — rare and fair.
The tests go green, the build stands tall and true,
A small bright victory in shades of blue.

So here's to late nights, coffee-stained and worn,
To every "why won't this compile?" dawn,
To code that's messy, code that's clean,
To the quiet joy of building something from a dream.
